Hospitality / Content System

Making Sholay's content feel as good as the experience.

Turning low-quality visual content into a more polished, cinematic content system built for attention.

Overview

The brief

Sholay is a hospitality brand that wanted its digital content to reflect the quality and character of the in-person experience. The existing visual output was inconsistent and did not communicate the energy or identity of the brand clearly.

VibeNova was engaged to review the content approach, establish a clearer visual language, and build a repeatable content system that could run consistently across platforms.

Scope of work

  • Content audit and visual direction review
  • Brand content guidelines and visual framework
  • Cinematic short-form content planning
  • Reel and static post system
  • Caption and caption-structure templates
  • Platform posting rhythm and calendar
The challenge

Inconsistent visuals, unclear brand voice

Restaurants live and die by atmosphere and aesthetics. When the content looks rushed or generic, it undercuts the brand — even if the product is excellent. Sholay's content was not telling the right story.

Existing posts lacked visual consistency, the editing style varied, and captions did not guide the audience toward any clear action or emotion. Scroll-stopping content requires intent, not just good food photography.

  • Inconsistent colour grading and shot style
  • No clear visual hierarchy or brand cues
  • Caption style that did not match the brand tone
  • Reel editing that did not create enough visual tension
  • No repeatable system to maintain quality
The approach

A content system built for cinema, not just posts

Rather than produce one-off content, VibeNova built a framework that the Sholay team could execute consistently — with or without ongoing agency involvement.

The approach started with extracting the brand's existing visual identity and codifying it into a simple content guide: colour temperature, shot types, caption structure, and posting rhythm.

  • Visual identity extraction and content brief
  • Shot direction guidelines for reels and statics
  • Mood-matched colour-grading references
  • Caption frameworks for engagement and atmosphere
  • Monthly content calendar template
  • Story structure for short-form video

What changed

From ad-hoc posts to a repeatable system

Visual consistency

Content now follows a defined colour temperature and shot structure that feels distinctly Sholay across every format.

Reel quality

Short-form videos follow a tighter edit structure — opening hook, atmosphere, and a clear close — rather than generic clips.

Repeatable workflow

A monthly content calendar and caption templates mean the team can execute consistently without reinventing the approach each time.

Brand voice

Captions now reflect the brand character — cinematic, warm, and grounded — rather than generic food-industry copy.
