Automotive Lubricants / E-commerce Growth

A sharper conversion review for a brand built on performance.

A conversion and growth review for an established automotive lubricants brand with a Shopify presence — finding the gaps, mapping the fixes, and outlining a clearer path forward.

Overview

The brief

Pakelo India is the Indian arm of Pakelo Motor Oil, an established European lubricants brand with a strong product range and a Shopify e-commerce presence. The brand had existing traffic and a customer base, but the digital journey — from discovery to purchase — had gaps that were costing conversions.

VibeNova was engaged to review the Shopify store, map the customer journey, identify the highest-impact conversion issues, and outline an actionable growth direction for search, email, and lead flow.

Scope of work

  • Shopify store conversion and UX audit
  • CRO (conversion rate optimisation) review
  • Email capture and list-building assessment
  • WhatsApp lead integration review
  • Dealer acquisition and B2B journey mapping
  • SEO and search direction planning
The challenge

An established brand with an unclear online path to purchase

Pakelo India had a credible product and a Shopify store — but the site was not structured to move visitors efficiently from discovery to decision. The brand also had two distinct audiences: direct retail customers and B2B dealer enquiries, each with different needs and different journeys.

Bridging those two audiences, closing the leaks in the retail conversion path, and building the foundations for sustained search growth were the core challenges of the engagement.

  • Retail conversion path had friction points across product and checkout pages
  • Email capture and re-marketing were underbuilt
  • WhatsApp and lead follow-up were disconnected from the store
  • Dealer / B2B enquiry journey was unclear and hard to find
  • SEO structure needed review and prioritisation
The approach

Audit first. Prioritise what moves the needle.

Rather than jumping to redesign or ads, VibeNova started with a structured review of what was already working and what was not. The goal was to give the Pakelo India team a clear, prioritised roadmap — not a list of generic recommendations.

The audit covered the full customer journey across both audience types, with specific attention to where visitors were dropping off and what system improvements would have the most direct impact on conversion and lead flow.

  • Full Shopify store CRO and UX review
  • Customer journey mapping for retail and dealer audiences
  • Email capture and automation recommendations
  • WhatsApp lead routing review and integration plan
  • B2B/dealer landing page and journey recommendations
  • SEO priority list and content direction
