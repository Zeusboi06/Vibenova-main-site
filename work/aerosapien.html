Aerospace & Defence

A digital presence built for a precision industry.

A premium digital presence for a precision-focused aerospace and defence manufacturer — credible, technical, and built to open the right doors.

Overview

The brief

Aerosapien is an aerospace and defence manufacturer whose products demand credibility and technical authority. When a potential partner or procurement team visits a company's website, their first read of the site determines whether the conversation continues.

VibeNova was engaged to build a corporate website and establish the digital foundations — Google presence, analytics, and search structure — that the brand needed to operate at a professional level online.

Scope of work

  • Premium corporate website design and build
  • Product and R&D page development
  • Technical SEO structure and on-page foundations
  • Google Analytics 4 setup and configuration
  • Google Search Console setup and verification
  • Domain and hosting configuration
The challenge

Precision industry. High-stakes first impression.

Aerospace and defence procurement is relationship-driven and highly scrutinised. A website that looks incomplete, generic, or technically shallow signals the wrong things to the wrong people.

Aerosapien needed a site that matched the precision and seriousness of its manufacturing work — built to inform, not to entertain, and to function well technically in search.

  • No existing web presence that matched the brand's technical level
  • Products and R&D output needed clear, structured presentation
  • Technical SEO foundations had to be built from the ground up
  • Analytics and Search Console were absent, leaving no visibility data
  • Design language had to feel authoritative without being generic
The approach

Build the digital layer that matches the product layer.

VibeNova started with a structured brief — mapping the company's products, capabilities, and audience to determine how the site should be architected and what each page needed to communicate.

The design direction prioritised clean technical presentation: dark backgrounds, strong typographic hierarchy, precise spacing, and a minimal palette that allowed the product and R&D work to lead.

  • Structured information architecture and page planning
  • Technical, editorial design direction
  • Product and R&D section built for clarity and depth
  • SEO meta structure, heading hierarchy, and schema markup
  • GA4 and Search Console properly connected and verified
  • Mobile-responsive build optimised for professional use
